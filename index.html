Initial Design Review

Selected Design Concept

Our DROID (Dynamic Rolling Olympian in Disguise) was designed to autonomously play a head-to-head game of curling on a 4 ft × 16 ft particle board arena. Starting from a 13" × 13" zone at one end, the robot must locate the house (bull's-eye) at the center seam of the field using IR beacons (909 Hz, 10" above surface), then slide up to 8 steel pucks (2.08" diameter, 8 oz) as close to the button as possible within a 2-minute match.
